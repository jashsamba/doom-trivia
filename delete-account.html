Doom Trivia – account and data deletion

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open Doom Trivia and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile (top right of the home screen).
  3. Tap Delete account, type DELETE, and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: by email

If you can't use the app, email doomtrivia@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Doom Trivia account". We will delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in (email, and your Google name and picture if you used Google)
  • Your username
  • Your stats and saved games
  • Your match history, including your answers (matches you played are removed for both players)

Nothing is kept after deletion. We do not keep backups of deleted accounts beyond our hosting provider's short-term disaster-recovery backups, which expire automatically.

See also our privacy policy.
