Doom Trivia – Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Doom Trivia is a trivia game for Android and the web. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. In short: we collect only what is needed to run your account and your games, we do not show ads, we do not use tracking or analytics tools, and we never sell your data.

What we collect

InformationWhenWhy
Email addressWhen you sign in with Google or an email codeTo create and secure your account
Name and profile picture from GoogleOnly if you sign in with GoogleShown on your own Profile page
UsernameCreated automatically at first sign-in; you can change itThe name other players see in matches
Game results (mode, topic, score, questions answered)When you play while signed inTo save and resume games and show your stats
Match data (your opponent, your answers and answer times, the winner)When you play a matchTo run live matches and show your win/loss record
Ding Dong DOOM room data (who is in the room, buzzes, answers, scores)When you play Ding Dong DOOMTo run the buzzer game and show the end-of-game recap to the players in that room
Progress (which questions you answered and whether they were right, coins, medals, Hard unlocks)When you play while signed inMedals, coins, your stats and the leaderboards
Country (a two-letter code, e.g. "CA")Worked out from your internet connection when you open your Profile or the leaderboard, at most once a dayCountry leaderboards. We store only the country code, never your IP address or precise location
Avatar choiceIf you pick one of the app's built-in avatarsShown next to your username

Playing as a guest stores no personal information on our servers; guest progress is not saved.

On your device, the app stores your sign-in session and your settings (such as day/night mode, sound, vibration and language).

While the app is open it also shares, without any name or account, which part of the game it is in (menu, solo, Play vs or Ding Dong DOOM) so the app can show how many players are online. This is not stored.

The app does not use GPS or ask for your location. It never uploads photos.

Who can see it

  • Other players can see your username and the results of matches and Ding Dong DOOM rooms you play with them.
  • Signed-in players can see the leaderboards: the username, avatar, country flag and points of the top players (and their own rank).
  • Your email, Google name and picture are never shown to other players.
  • We use Supabase to host the app's database and sign-in, and Google to provide "Continue with Google". They process data only to provide these services to us.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

How it is protected

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access rules ensure each player can read and change only their own account data.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: Profile → Delete account. Your account, username, stats, saved games, match history, coins, medals, avatar and country are deleted immediately and permanently.
  • Without the app: follow the steps on our account deletion page.

Children

Doom Trivia is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Trivia content

Questions are written with the help of AI models and checked before they are published. Your personal information is never sent to these AI services.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Questions or requests: doomtrivia@gmail.com.
